COACH LOL IA PRIVACY AND LICENSE

Coach LoL IA is free and open source (MIT license, below).
What data it uses:
- It reads your LoL client and the in-game API on your own PC (champion select, current game, match history). Your profile, history and analyses are stored only on this PC.
- For builds, the Coach LoL IA server only receives the champion, role and mode you look up.
- Loading screen: the region, champions, roles and public Riot identifier (PUUID) of the players are sent to request their rank from the official Riot API. Your Riot ID is not sent and nothing is stored.
- AI coach (analysis, tips and "Your coach"): numbers from your games, without your name or Riot ID, are sent to our server and our AI provider (Anthropic). Nothing is stored.
How to turn it off: Settings → AI coach (analysis, tips and pep talk) and Settings → Loading screen. Offline, the app keeps working with what is saved.
Full policy: https://www.coachlolia.com/en/privacy
Uninstall: Windows Settings → Apps → Coach LoL IA → Uninstall.
