# 👋 Welcome to Coach LoL IA!

This is the official community of **Coach LoL IA**, a **free** Windows app for League of Legends:
- 📦 **Builds and runes** from Emerald+ games with their stats, imported into the client in one click.
- 🖥️ In-game **overlay** with widgets and a **voice coach** in 5 languages.
- 🧠 **AI analysis** of your games, **challenges** and the **"Your coach"** chat in your Profile.

**Getting started**
1. Read the <#reglas> (rules, they are short).
2. Pick your language, region, rank and roles in **Channels & Roles** (open the server name menu). See <#roles>.
3. Get the app in <#descargas-y-faq> or at https://www.coachlolia.com/en/download
4. Something wrong? Ask in <#ayuda> or open a thread in <#soporte-y-bugs>. English chat: <#english>.

A new challenge drops every Monday in <#retos-de-la-semana>. See you on the Rift! 🏆
